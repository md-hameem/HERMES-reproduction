## What do the verified author policies imply for submission planning?

### Takeaway

Novelty, relevant experimental support and fit are explicit editorial requirements. The immediate publication strategy is to build a compact, defensible original contribution and reserve revision time and possible publication charges.

### Cited Findings

- TMM requires a clear problem, significant contribution, close related work and an explanation of what is new. Its policy names lack of novelty, straightforward combinations of established methods and insufficient experimental data as reasons for immediate rejection. Initial regular manuscripts may not exceed ten double-column pages under the retrieved instructions. [TMM author policy](https://signalprocessingsociety.org/publications-resources/information-tmm-authors)
- The same current TMM page lists voluntary charges and mandatory USD 220 for each page beyond eight published pages. OA charges are separate. These are the retrieved publisher terms and should be rechecked for the submission date and publication format. [TMM charges](https://signalprocessingsociety.org/publications-resources/information-tmm-authors)
- TCSVT's retrieved instructions offer traditional publication without an OA fee. They list mandatory overlength charges beyond eleven pages for a Transactions paper when voluntary page charges are not honored, with rates depending on CAS membership. Recheck the live submission portal rather than treating these terms as a complete future quotation. [TCSVT author policy](https://ieee-cas.org/tcsvt-submission-manuscript)
- TCSVT considers original work, surveys/reviews and appropriately expanded conference articles. TMM expressly encourages reproducibility materials. [TCSVT publication scope](https://ieee-cas.org/publication/tcsvt) [TMM reproducibility guidance](https://signalprocessingsociety.org/publications-resources/ieee-transactions-multimedia)

### Inferences

- For this user's requirement, the original-method article is the relevant submission route. The audited reproduction, null FIFO comparison and attention diagnostic can motivate and support its experimental design.
- A baseline correction, cache-budget sweep or additional graph of the existing results needs a distinct scientific contribution before becoming a convincing journal method article.
- Prepare a contribution-to-evidence table for the eventual paper. Every claimed improvement should identify its hypothesis, closest competing method, controlled test, uncertainty measure and computational cost.
- Keep time for review, revision and a subsequent submission if required. The user has not supplied a deadline, so a calendar can currently describe work phases rather than guarantee a graduation-compatible acceptance date.
- Optional OA expenditure should be separated from compute spending and any mandatory overlength fees. An institution may have an agreement affecting APC payment, which must be checked locally.
