## The baseline study supports a focused scientific question

The project has substantial research assets. These include pinned model/source environments, raw predictions, per-video checkpoints, statistical analysis, timing records, failure examples and independent integrity checks. The public StreamingBench annotations and the project's generated experiment outputs have different ownership and contribution roles. The latter support reproducibility and analysis. An original-dataset claim would require a separately designed and documented dataset contribution. [Current project status](../PROJECT_STATUS.md), [Evidence index](../../evidence/README.md)

The full real-time visual comparison contains **498 videos and 2,495 questions per method**. HERMES 4K answers 1,555 correctly and FIFO 4K answers 1,546, or 62.3246% and 61.9639%. The +0.3607-percentage-point difference has a video-cluster 95% interval of −0.4810 to +1.1628 points. The result motivates a question about when semantic selection helps. It leaves a general accuracy advantage unestablished for this configuration. The aggregate also reuses October samples 201–250 across sessions and hardware, which belongs in any manuscript description of these measurements. [Full paired results](../../results/five_stage/full_benchmark_paired.csv), [Local evidence audit](../../evidence/audits/five_stage_local_audit.json)

The most specific implementation lead is the scorer approximation. The current manual path holds pseudo-query embeddings fixed across layers and omits a causal mask. On one real 16-frame prefix, its attention has mean cosine 0.5593 against the native reference, and selected-token Jaccard is 0.6503 at budget 256. This establishes a selection discrepancy on that prefix. A causal reference can compute attention faithfully while still supplying an imperfect estimate of future-question relevance, so QA outcomes need their own experiment. A generic description such as adaptive memory, entropy-triggered recall, multiple pseudo-questions or temporal coverage has extensive overlap. The earlier EDMI proposal should remain a historical hypothesis until a mechanism and a specific novelty argument are established. The local literature catalogue is useful preparation, while the submission needs close comparisons with the few methods that share its mechanism. | Closest published work | Relevant overlap | Consequence for the new method |
|---|---|---|

| SAVEMem | Pseudo-question bank, bounded tiered memory and recency gating | A query bank plus recent/older-memory split requires further differentiation. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.07897v1) |

The preferred **provisional candidate** is a method that reuses causally propagated generic-probe states between streaming updates and refreshes them when an online staleness criterion predicts that reuse will harm selection. First obtain fresh causal scoring as an experimental reference. After that pass, cache the small layer-wise contextual query states. Score later visual-cache updates through cheaper query/key products while monitoring whether those states remain useful. A predeclared recent-token floor can protect immediate evidence. The future real question remains unavailable to the maintenance rule.

The research contribution would be the refresh criterion and its behavior as the stream and compressed cache change. It would need a defensible derivation or calibration procedure, a comparison with fixed-interval refresh, measured selection fidelity and a useful QA–compression-cost result. The distinction to investigate is contextual propagated-query reuse during continual compression, relative to SAVEMem's reused semantic prior and KV²'s sparse reconstruction. The targeted search has not established that distinction as novel. The nearest equations, implementations and later versions need a focused comparison before investing in a full implementation. [SAVEMem](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.07897v1), [KV²](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.03198v2), [StreamMem](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.15717v1)

Several correctness conditions are part of the method. HERMES changes its local/global pseudo-question text after an answered question by incorporating conversation history, so text changes must invalidate or separately key cached probe states. RoPE logical positions evolve, and compaction changes their alignment. Cached rotated query projections require the appropriate transform or refresh. Eviction changes the set of visual entries, so scores need stable token identities or explicit invalidation. Auxiliary cached states, trigger computation and synchronizations belong in the memory and timing budget. A criterion that merely correlates with token-set agreement supplies no demonstrated bound on answer error. [Current HERMES implementation](../../inference/llavaov_hermes.py)

The falsifiable hypothesis is that, under a fixed cache and scoring-compute budget, selective refresh retains more of the fresh causal reference's useful QA behavior than fixed-interval refresh. Original HERMES and FIFO establish practical relevance. Fresh causal scoring itself needs a preliminary QA/evidence-preservation signal. If it changes selected tokens without a promising answer-quality result, the main motivation for this candidate weakens and the effort should pause.

Two fallback directions deserve only conditional investigation. Repeated summary consolidation could be studied for loss of support mass or event identity, but standard weighted aggregation, ProtoKV and Video-MemComp are close controls. Adaptive allocation between temporal sampling and retention could address evidence missed at 0.5 fps, but the required observation loss must first be demonstrated and the encoded-frame budget held equal. Existing input filtering, temporal redundancy removal and multi-granularity methods create substantial overlap, and this direction expands the experimental problem. [ProtoKV](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/minh26a.html), [Video-MemComp](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32784), [InfiniPot-V](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.15745), [MuKV](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Xiao_MuKV_Multi-Grained_KV_Cache_Compression_for_Long_Streaming_Video_Question-Answering_CVPR_2026_paper.html)

A few days can establish a credible pilot decision while leaving further confirmation for another allocation. A small pilot demonstrates mechanism and feasibility at its tested scope. It cannot carry a broad claim solely through a favorable point estimate.

Record accuracy, paired differences, refresh frequency, compression time, peak allocated and reserved memory, and tail event latency. Keep nested timers separate. Compare at identical frame sampling, decoding settings, precision, cache accounting and input prefixes. Use fixed-interval refresh and the fresh causal reference to isolate the benefit of the proposed trigger. A later additional-model or benchmark result strengthens generality when it tests a real failure mode, and its scale should reflect the claim and budget.

The go/no-go decision should examine evidence recovery and the quality/cost frontier. A small pilot is suited to diagnosing mechanism and feasibility. Continue if the proposed mechanism is active, the identified support survives more effectively, the quality/cost result is promising, and failures elsewhere are understood. Revise or stop if faithful scoring adds no useful signal, the trigger costs as much as refresh, the gain disappears under matched controls, or the improvement is explained by extra memory or frames. The method can then be narrowed, or the next hypothesis can follow the observed failure mechanism.

## Submission quality comes from an auditable contribution

The eventual manuscript should state one central claim in its opening and attach every major claim to evidence. Its introduction can use the current FIFO comparison and scorer discrepancy to motivate the problem. The method section should define the cached state, update/refresh operations, causality constraints, memory accounting and computational cost. The related-work section should compare the mechanism directly with StreamMem, SAVEMem, KV² and the other nearest methods. The experiments should isolate the mechanism, quantify accuracy and resource trade-offs, and show the conditions under which it succeeds or fails. This structure follows the emphasis on contribution, rigor and supported claims in the retrieved journal and conference guidance. [TMM editorial requirements](https://signalprocessingsociety.org/publications-resources/information-tmm-authors), [CVPR review criteria](https://cvpr.thecvf.com/Conferences/2027/ReviewerGuidelines), [ACM MM review criteria](https://2026.acmmm.org/site/review-process-guidelines.html)

A convincing paper can pursue improved accuracy at a fixed budget, comparable quality at lower scoring cost, or a well-characterized operating regime. The selected claim must survive the appropriate controls and uncertainty analysis. A modest point estimate accompanied by a clear mechanism and reliable supporting evidence has a different scientific meaning from an uncontrolled gain. The present negative or inconclusive findings can strengthen the paper's motivation and limitations when described accurately. The new mechanism carries the original-method claim your professor requires.

Prepare reviewer-facing artifacts alongside the experiments. These should include the precise code revision, environment and model pins, configurations, selection traces for interpretable examples, raw per-question outputs, paired statistics and an easy-to-run minimal example. Preserve failed or infeasible controls with an explanation. Distinguish measured GPU operations from whole-pipeline time, and declare the original study's reuse across hardware. The existing repository organization is well suited to retaining this provenance. [Current artifact audit](../../evidence/audits/five_stage_local_audit.json)

Journal/conference extension policies must be checked before planning sequential publications from related versions. Reserve time for revision and a subsequent submission if needed. Submission, a revise decision, formal acceptance, online publication and indexing are distinct milestones, and the professor's guidance or applicable university policy must identify which milestone satisfies graduation.
